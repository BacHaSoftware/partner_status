Partner Status

Partner Status by Bac Ha Software is an Odoo add-on designed to help users effectively manage contact statuses by assigning contacts to archived state. Developed as an extension of the core Contact module, this application helps businesses maintain secure communication channels and prevent unintended interactions with restricted parties.

Perfect for: Sales & CRM Teams, Procurement Departments, Customer Support Specialists.

Key Features

Partner Status

In the Odoo contact view, this module enhances the existing Customer Status field by adding a new "Archived" status. Once a contact is moved to this status, it will automatically be hidden and will not appear in any selection lists.
